Welcome to Pagnetic. This walkthrough shows the merchant and shopper experience that Shopify reviewers can reproduce after installing the app. Pagnetic helps a merchant match an approved product-page message to the promise in an advertising campaign, while keeping Shopify in control of the product, price, inventory, cart, and checkout.

The journey can begin before installation. A merchant enters one public Shopify product URL and, optionally, the exact message from an ad. Pagnetic reads only the public product content needed for the preview. It identifies a specific messaging opportunity and shows an original version, a source-grounded proposed change, and an alternative. The preview is an evidence review. It does not promise sales lift, and submitting this form does not start billing.

After installation, Pagnetic uses the merchant's existing Shopify admin session. There is no separate Pagnetic account. The merchant selects a product from the synced catalog. Pagnetic then shows the current source, its diagnosis, and the exact proposed message. The source appears beside the proposal so the merchant can check every statement. Pagnetic abstains when the product content cannot support a useful and distinct message.

The merchant remains responsible for visible content. They can edit the proposed headline and supporting line, preview it in context, and approve the exact version they want to test. Content approval is recorded separately from test activation and separately from subscription approval. This prevents an approved sentence from silently becoming a live experiment or a charge.

Next, Pagnetic guides the merchant through the Online Store theme step. The merchant opens Shopify's theme editor, adds the Pagnetic panel to the relevant published product template, previews it, and saves the theme. Pagnetic then checks the published storefront rather than trusting an unsaved preview. Until that check passes, shoppers continue to receive the original product page.

The shopper experience stays inside the normal Shopify storefront. Pagnetic can show the approved universal message or a campaign-matched message above the native product form. Variant choice, quantity, selling plans, add to cart, accelerated checkout, Shop Pay, prices, inventory, shipping, taxes, and payment remain Shopify-owned. If a message is missing, stale, or not approved, Pagnetic immediately falls back to the original experience.

Before activation, Pagnetic verifies the required measurement surfaces, including the theme block, Web Pixel events, standard checkout, consent behavior, and the original fallback. The Overview page presents one clear next step and identifies whether the merchant or Pagnetic owns it. A merchant can pause serving at any time, which returns all new shoppers to the original storefront.

During a test, assignment remains stable and Pagnetic reconciles browser events with Shopify orders and refunds. Results compare revenue per eligible session. Pagnetic does not change prices or discounts, choose a winner from a weak result, or claim revenue that cannot be supported by the recorded data. The Results page explains whether evidence is conclusive and lets the merchant keep, revise, or stop within the approved workflow.

Pagnetic's Founding Beta plan is forty-nine US dollars per month with a thirty-day trial. Shopify hosts plan selection and subscription approval. Development stores and Shopify reviewers can test the flow at no charge. The Settings page shows the server-verified subscription state, supported-surface evidence, pause control, data export, privacy information, and support contact.

To reproduce this review, install Pagnetic, open it from Shopify admin, sync the catalog, select a product, review and approve a source-grounded message, add and save the theme block, verify the storefront, and complete a Shopify test order. Then inspect measurement and use Pause to confirm the original storefront returns. Pagnetic is designed to make campaign-message testing controlled, measurable, and reversible from end to end.
